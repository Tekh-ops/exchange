### Track 3: Learning / Adaptive Behavior

### The Petty Sorcerer
*Every spell leaves a memory.*

### Idea
A turn-based pixel-art wizard RPG built with React, TypeScript, Vite, Zustand, and PixiJS. Enemies track your casting habits, predict your next element, adapt their resistances, punish repetitive tactics, and shift emotional states—culminating in multi-phase boss encounters.

### How to Play
- **Cast Spells:** Click a spell card or press `1`–`7` to cast. Toggle **Charge** to empower chargeable spells on the next turn.
- **Lanes & Dodging:** Shift between `Left`, `Center`, and `Right` lanes using stamina, or use **Quickstep**, **Perfect Dodge**, and **Arcane Blink** to evade telegraphed attacks.
- **Voice Casting (Optional):** Press the microphone button in Chrome/Edge and speak a spell or action name (e.g., *"Cast Petty Spark"*).
- **Progression:** Earn XP from battles to level up, allocate attribute points (`Vitality`, `Intelligence`, `Mind`, `Dexterity`, `Arcane`), unlock spells and gear, and challenge **Vorath, Arch-Lich of the Eternal Crown**.
